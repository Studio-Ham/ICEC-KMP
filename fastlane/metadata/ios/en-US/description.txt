ICEC (Mosaic Editor Creator) is a photo editor that detects faces on your device.

Key Features
- On-device face detection: Select a photo from your device. ICEC detects faces on your device.
- Selective square mosaic: Select which faces to cover with square pixel mosaic.
- Zoom and pan: Zoom and pan the photo to check and select each face.
- Original preservation: ICEC saves a new photo at original resolution. ICEC keeps your original photo unchanged.
- No metadata: The saved photo contains no metadata, including GPS data.

Privacy
- ICEC has no accounts, no login, no advertisements, no payments, no developer servers, and no developer analytics tools.
- ICEC does not send your photos or face data to the developer or external servers.
- ICEC runs Google ML Kit on your device for face detection.
- Google ML Kit may send SDK usage statistics and performance metrics to Google. Google receives no photos or face data.

Supported Languages
- Korean, English
